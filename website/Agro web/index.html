Tomato - Infection Detected

Tomato Early Blight

Early blight is caused by the fungus Alternaria solani. It appears as brown spots with concentric rings on leaves, eventually causing leaves to turn yellow and drop off.

45%
Severity Index
Moderate damage. Quick spraying recommended.
AI Confidence Score 94%

AI Treatment Prescription

Organic Remedies: Apply Neem oil spray. Prune lower infected leaves to prevent ground transmission.

Chemical Option: Spray Mancozeb or Copper Oxychloride fungicide at 2g per liter dilution.

Watering recommendation: Reduce overhead watering. Switch to drip irrigation to keep leaf surfaces dry.
